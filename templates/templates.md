## Tokyo Midnight Walk (Sora, Cinematic)

```
A stylish woman walks down a Tokyo street filled with warm glowing neon and animated city signage. She wears a black leather jacket, a long red dress, and black boots. The street is damp and reflective, creating a mirror effect of the colorful lights. Many pedestrians walk about. Camera: Slow dolly in following the subject. Lighting: Golden hour warm tones mixed with neon accents. Style: Photorealistic 8K, shallow depth of field. Duration: 10 seconds.
```

Tips: Golden hour + neon creates beautiful contrast. Add rain puddles for extra reflection depth. Specify clothing colors for visual pop.
